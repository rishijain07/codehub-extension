CodeHub Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes information handled by the CodeHub browser extension and the CodeHub marketing website, how it is used and disclosed, and your privacy choices.

By using the CodeHub website or browser extension, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions:
- Extension refers to CodeHub, the open-source browser extension.
- Website refers to the CodeHub marketing website at https://rishijain07.github.io/codehub-extension/.
- Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
- Usage Data refers to data generated by use of the Website or Extension.
- You means the individual accessing or using the Website or Extension.

2. Collecting and Using Your Data

Types of Data Collected:
A. Personal Data:
- GitHub Username: Stored locally in your browser to identify your repository owner and display your profile in the Extension.
- GitHub Authentication Token: Secure OAuth/access token generated during the GitHub authorization process, stored locally in your browser storage (`chrome.storage.local` / `browser.storage.local`) to authenticate commits and file uploads directly to your GitHub account.
- Codeforces Handle (Optional): Stored locally in your browser to fetch and sync your public Codeforces submissions.
- HackerRank challenge metadata: Public challenge pages, accepted submission code, titles, difficulty, tags, and statements are read locally in the browser. Private hiring assessments, tests, and interview pages are not synchronized.
- Temporary submission attempts: For AtCoder, InterviewBit, and AlgoExpert, the Extension temporarily stores the submitted source code, language, problem key, submission time, account identifier used to match the result, linked repository, and submission/execution ID when available. Attempts are scoped to the browser tab, expire after ten minutes, and are cleared on rejection, tab closure, or linked-repository change. AlgoExpert's account subject is SHA-256 hashed in the page bridge before it is passed to the Extension.
- Website analytics: The marketing website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID `G-9VYN9BL7XX`) to understand website traffic. Google Analytics may receive page URL and referrer, IP address, browser/device information, approximate location, usage events, and cookie or similar identifiers as described in Google's [partner-site privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Analytics data collection documentation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727).
- The Extension does not collect platform passwords or copy platform cookies into its storage. Platform account access uses the session already held by your browser.

B. Problem & Submission Data:
- Code submissions, problem titles, difficulty ratings, tags, and problem descriptions from supported platforms:
  * LeetCode (https://leetcode.com)
  * GeeksforGeeks (https://geeksforgeeks.org)
  * Codeforces (https://codeforces.com)
  * CodeChef (https://www.codechef.com)
  * NeetCode (https://neetcode.io)
  * HackerRank public challenges and contests (https://www.hackerrank.com)
  * Code360 by Coding Ninjas: https://www.naukri.com/code360/problems/ and supported problem pages on https://www.codingninjas.com
  * AtCoder (https://atcoder.jp)
  * InterviewBit (https://www.interviewbit.com)
  * AlgoExpert (https://www.algoexpert.io)
- Code360 data includes submitted source code, programming language, problem identifier and URL, submission identifier and verdict, title, statement, difficulty, and available tags. CodeHub observes problem submissions and checks their final result before syncing accepted solutions.
- AtCoder, InterviewBit, and AlgoExpert sync new submissions only; they do not backfill historical submissions. AtCoder data does not include a difficulty rating. AlgoExpert's native Very Hard label is retained in problem details and grouped under Hard in aggregate statistics.

3. How We Use Your Data

Extension data is used to deliver the core features of the Extension:
- Automatically extracting your accepted solutions and pushing them to your designated GitHub repository.
- Generating markdown documentation (README.md) with difficulty and topic tags alongside your code in your GitHub repository.
- Displaying real-time problem-solving analytics, difficulty breakdowns, and platform activity share on your CodeHub Dashboard and Popup.

Website analytics data is used to understand visits and improve the marketing website.

4. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

- Browser Storage: Tokens, handles, preferences, cached statistics, and temporary AtCoder, InterviewBit, and AlgoExpert submission attempts are stored in browser extension storage (`chrome.storage.local` / `browser.storage.local`). These pending attempts expire after ten minutes and are cleared when rejected, when their tab closes, or when the linked repository changes. Progress statistics are also written to your selected GitHub repository.
- Code360 Temporary Storage: Submission tracking information, captured source code, and problem metadata may be stored in the problem tab's `sessionStorage` to track a submission across page reloads. This storage belongs to the platform page's origin and is normally cleared when the tab is closed.
- Extension Backend: The Extension does not operate a CodeHub upload server, hosted database, or intermediary service. Extension sync requests go directly from your browser to the platform and GitHub services described below.
- Website Analytics: The separate marketing website loads Google Analytics 4 to measure website use. The browser sends analytics data directly to Google; Google's processing is described in its [privacy information for sites using Google services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). This website analytics is separate from the Extension's solution synchronization.
- GitHub API: Code submissions and repository files are transmitted directly from your browser to the official GitHub API (https://api.github.com) using your authenticated token.
- Codeforces API: Public submission metadata is fetched directly from the official Codeforces API (https://codeforces.com/api) using the handle you provide.
- HackerRank: Public challenge pages are read directly from HackerRank. When fullscreen mode hides the difficulty sidebar, CodeHub may request the canonical public problem URL without the fullscreen query parameter to read that metadata. This data is sent to GitHub only when you submit an accepted public challenge.
- Code360: The extension reads supported problem pages and requests problem details and submission verdicts directly from the platform's API using your existing same-origin login session. Your Code360 session credentials are not sent to GitHub. After acceptance is confirmed, source code, generated documentation, and statistics are uploaded directly to your selected GitHub repository under `codingninjas/`.
- AtCoder, InterviewBit, and AlgoExpert: Submission and result data are read from supported pages using your existing browser session. After a matching successful result is confirmed, the submitted source, generated documentation, and statistics are uploaded directly to your selected GitHub repository. The extension does not send your platform session credentials to GitHub.
- Website Analytics: Google Analytics may use first-party cookies or similar identifiers and process information it receives under Google's terms and privacy policy. The website does not use these analytics records to access your coding-platform accounts or GitHub repository.
- We do not sell or rent your data or use extension submission data for advertising. Extension data is shared with GitHub and supported platforms only as described above to provide synchronization.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

- You retain complete ownership and control over your data.
- You can unlink your repository at any time via the Extension Dashboard or Popup, which removes repository configurations from local storage.
- Pending AtCoder, InterviewBit, and AlgoExpert attempts expire after ten minutes and are cleared when rejected, when their tab closes, or when their linked repository changes. These attempt records are removed when the Extension's browser storage is removed or the Extension is uninstalled.
- You can use the "Reset Stats" feature to clear locally cached problem statistics and delete `stats.json` tracking files from your GitHub repository.
- Uninstalling the Extension instantly deletes all local storage data, tokens, and settings associated with CodeHub from your browser.
- Uninstalling does not delete files or commit history already uploaded to GitHub, or platform-origin tab storage. Close Code360 tabs or clear the platform's site data to remove temporary tab storage. Manage uploaded files and repository visibility directly on GitHub; public repositories can expose your code and metadata to others.
- You can manage Google Analytics collection through browser privacy settings or Google's [Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). See Google's privacy policy for its retention and processing practices.

6. Security

CodeHub uses HTTPS for communication with GitHub, supported coding platforms, and Google Analytics on the marketing website. Extension tokens and settings remain in your browser's extension storage; the marketing website analytics tag does not receive the extension's token or solution files.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Extension does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy and within the extension repository.
